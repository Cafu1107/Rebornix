Onb_P1_Title=Reinstalling Windows, without the headache
Onb_P1_Intro=Rebornix backs up the important things to a second drive before you reinstall, and puts them all back in the right order afterwards. This short guide walks you through it step by step.
Onb_P1_Steps=Back up: BEFORE reinstalling, saves your drivers, Wi-Fi passwords, app list, game saves and Windows settings.\nRestore: AFTER reinstalling, brings it all back in order: drivers → Wi-Fi → apps → game saves → settings.\nQuick Install: installs apps like Chrome, Steam and Discord on a clean PC in one click.\nRebornix needs no installation; it's a single .exe file that keeps its settings and logs in its own folder.
Onb_P1_Tip=You can open this guide any time with the "How does it work?" button on the Home page.
Onb_P2_Badge=Before you start
Onb_P2_Title=Put it in the right place first
Onb_P2_Intro=A reinstall wipes the C: drive completely. Your backup and Rebornix itself must live somewhere that won't be erased.
Onb_P2_Steps=Copy Rebornix.exe to a second drive (e.g. D:), an external drive or a USB stick and run it from there.\nChoose that drive as the backup target too. If you pick C:, the app stops you with a big red warning.\nMake sure there is enough free space (game saves can take a few GB).\nUsing it for the first time? Turn on Settings → Dry run: you'll see what would happen without changing anything.\nThe app asks for administrator permission when it starts; it needs this for drivers and Wi-Fi.
Onb_P2_Tip=Before reinstalling, open the backup folder (e.g. D:\Rebornix) and make sure you see the Drivers, WiFi, Data and Backups folders inside.
Onb_P3_Badge=BEFORE reinstall · Step 1
Onb_P3_Title=What should I do on the Back up page?
Onb_P3_Intro=Open "Back up" from the menu on the left and follow these steps in order:
Onb_P3_Steps=Backup target: choose your second drive. The backup is written to the Rebornix folder on it.\nDrivers: leave it on. Network/Wi-Fi drivers are marked "Critical"; you need them to get online after the reinstall.\nWi-Fi passwords: turn on if you want. Your saved network passwords are encrypted with a password you choose. Don't forget it!\nApp list: leave it on. Which programs are installed is recorded and they are installed automatically after the reinstall.\nGame saves: press "Scan games" and untick the ones you don't want. Add save folders it can't find with "Add folder".\nWindows settings: theme, wallpaper, taskbar, mouse and similar settings are saved.\nPress "Start backup" and check that the summary at the end shows no errors.
Onb_P3_Tip=The backup never deletes or changes your files; it only reads and copies them.
Onb_P4_Badge=BEFORE reinstall · Step 2
Onb_P4_Title=Back up what Rebornix doesn't
Onb_P4_Intro=Rebornix doesn't copy your personal files automatically. Before reinstalling, secure these yourself:
Onb_P4_Steps=Personal folders: copy Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Videos, Music and Downloads to a second or external drive.\nBrowser: sign in to Chrome/Edge/Firefox and turn on sync; that's how passwords and bookmarks come back.\nAccounts: make sure you have your authenticator (two-step verification) backup codes and know your important passwords.\nLicenses: note down Office, game and software license keys. Your Windows license is usually tied to your Microsoft account.\nIf you use BitLocker, save your recovery key (aka.ms/myrecoverykey).\nOther important files: email archives (Outlook .pst), projects that aren't in the cloud, phone backups.
Onb_P4_Tip=Once everything is copied, unplug the backup drive (if it's external) so it can't be wiped by accident during the reinstall.
Onb_P5_Badge=AFTER reinstall
Onb_P5_Title=Run these in order after Windows is installed
Onb_P5_Intro=Plug in your backup drive, open Rebornix.exe from the Rebornix folder and go to the "Restore" page. The backup is found automatically.
Onb_P5_Steps=Drivers: run this first. Network drivers are installed first. If a restart is needed, restart and open Rebornix again; it continues where it left off.\nWi-Fi: enter your backup password; your networks are added and the internet is checked. If it doesn't connect, pick your network from the list and press "Connect".\nApps: if you're online, the saved apps are installed silently one by one. Apps already installed are skipped.\nGame saves: run this after signing in to your game platforms (Steam etc.). On conflicts, "Keep the newer one" is recommended.\nWindows settings: the settings you choose come back. If you don't like them, undo on the "Windows Settings" page.\nCheck "Retry failed" and the "Install manually" list at the bottom.
Onb_P5_Tip=You can also press "Run all in order" to do everything at once; you'll be asked to confirm every important step.
